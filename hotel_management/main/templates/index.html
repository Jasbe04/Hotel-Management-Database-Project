Hotel Management System

Data entry forms based on the project ER diagram

Guest Room Category Room Reservation Invoice
Form Menu

1. Guest Form — registers a new guest (entity: guest)
2. Room Category Form — defines room types (entity: category)
3. Room Form — adds a room, linked to a category (entities: room, relationship: categorizes)
4. Reservation Form — books a room for a guest (entities: reservation, relationships: books, reserves)

Foreign keys (Guest, Room, Reservation, Category) are selected from dropdown lists loaded with sample data, as required by the assignment.
